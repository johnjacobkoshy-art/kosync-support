Kosync Privacy Policy

Policy dated November 25, 2026 · Covers Kosync 0.4.5 for iPhone and Wear OS and this support website

Kosync connects your iPhone and watch directly over Bluetooth. You can use the apps without creating a Kosync account. John Jacob Koshy develops Kosync and handles privacy requests at kosynced@gmail.com.

Your devices process the records used by the companion apps. Kosync has no developer-operated server for health records, notification content or media history. The apps have no advertising, third-party analytics or developer crash-upload service. Kosync does not sell personal information, track you across apps, or use health data for advertising or marketing.

Pairing and reconnecting

The apps use a saved watch identifier, the iPhone’s Bluetooth identifier for the watch, watch name and model, and connection times to recognize your watch. Battery level, charging state and signal information help show its status. Pairing uses the iPhone camera to scan the watch’s QR code, or you can enter the secret manually. The scanner processes the code on the phone without saving a photo or sending it to the developer.

The watch sends its persistent Kosync identifier at the start of a connection, before the app establishes encryption. The Bluetooth service allows that exchange without requiring an encrypted link. The QR secret authenticates pairing and derives separate keys for each direction. Kosync then protects its own health, status and command messages with AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption. Notifications and media use Apple’s separate Bluetooth services. Kosync requires a Bluetooth bond for those services and relies on the operating systems to protect their traffic.

Pairing secrets are protected by the device-only iPhone Keychain and, on the watch, by Android Keystore. Keep the QR code and manual key private. Devices retain pairing information so they can reconnect.

Health and fitness

When you enable supported health features and grant permission, the watch uses Wear OS Health Services for heart rate, step counts and walking or running distance where available. Kosync can record the time and duration of a walk you start in the watch app. Background heart-rate collection also depends on your watch’s background sensor permission. Kosync uses measurement times, watch identifiers and record IDs to preserve history and prevent repeat imports. The watch may also report daily total calories, which include resting and active energy. Kosync displays this total and does not write it to Apple Health as active calories. Kosync does not import your entire Samsung Health history, sleep stages or GPS routes.

The watch can keep up to 4,096 pending records in encrypted, app-private storage while disconnected. Acknowledged records leave the pending queue. Limited recent measurements and synchronization checkpoints can remain to support recovery. Pausing collection or losing paid access preserves queued data. On iPhone, Kosync can show the latest watch readings, cache a recent heart-rate reading and, with your permission, write supported heart-rate, step, distance and workout records to Apple Health. The dashboard can read authorized Kosync watch history from Apple Health. Your step goal and display preferences are stored locally.

Apple Health manages its own records and any synchronization with iCloud under your Apple settings. The iPhone’s latest heart-rate cache is excluded from backup. Ordinary preferences and saved watch profiles may be included in device backups. The watch app disables app backup. Platform backups and records you share using Apple Health follow your platform settings.

Notifications, incoming calls and media

If you enable system notification sharing, the watch can receive the originating app, notification title, preview, time, available actions and any caller information provided by iOS. These are used to display alerts and send supported actions back to the iPhone. Kosync does not read your address book or record call audio. The watch keeps up to 100 notification entries in session memory. An alert removed on the phone can remain marked inactive in watch history. Watch system notifications are also managed by Wear OS.

If you enable media sharing, Apple’s media service can provide the player, track, artist or album, playback state, position and supported controls to the watch. The watch can return current media details over the encrypted Kosync channel for iPhone controls. Kosync uses current session data and does not stream audio or maintain a listening-history database. Watch notifications and optional iPhone Live Activities can expose connection, battery or enabled media details to anyone who can see those screens. You can turn previews and Lock Screen details off.

Purchases, preferences and diagnostics

Apple processes App Store payments. Kosync uses Apple-verified transaction information, such as product, expiry and revocation, to determine Monthly, Lifetime and Supporter access. The watch receives a plan, membership and access expiry over the encrypted app channel. Kosync never receives your payment card details. If you start the optional nonbilling trial, a local record tracks its eligibility and timing. Your devices also store settings such as app appearance, and the iPhone stores the document version and time you acknowledged the terms.

For troubleshooting, the apps keep limited diagnostic events such as Bluetooth errors, retries, identifiers, queue counts and measurement timestamps. The operating systems can also keep logs or crash reports subject to their own settings. You can choose to share an iPhone diagnostic export. The recipient then receives the report you selected. Review it first because identifiers and times can reveal patterns of use.

Your controls and deletion choices

  • Health permissions and records. Turn off watch collection in Kosync or revoke its health and activity permissions through watch settings. Use Apple Health’s app permissions to change Kosync’s access. Revoking access stops future permitted use but leaves existing records. Use Apple Health to review or delete saved samples. On iPhone, Settings → Legal & Privacy → Permissions and privacy controls → Clear local health preview removes the Kosync preview.
  • Watch data and pairing. On the iPhone, open Devices, select the watch and choose Forget Watch to remove its saved iPhone pairing key, profile and related previews. To revoke the watch’s shared pairing secret, use the watch’s pairing reset. To erase Kosync’s local watch records, including pending health data, use the watch system settings to clear Kosync’s app storage. This also removes its setup and can discard records that have not reached Apple Health.
  • Alerts and media. In the watch’s Kosync notification settings, open Recent alerts and choose Clear local history. Manage system notifications separately. Use the watch’s preview settings and the iPhone’s Bluetooth system-notification setting where available. In Kosync’s iPhone privacy controls you can disable watch media, hide Lock Screen media or end a Live Activity.
  • Diagnostics and purchases. You can clear the iPhone’s in-app diagnostic log in its privacy controls. System logs, exported files and copies already sent have separate retention. Apple retains its purchase records. Removing Kosync or forgetting a watch does not cancel a subscription. You can manage it under Subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Email support and website visits

If you email the developer, your address, message and anything you attach go to John Jacob Koshy’s support mailbox, hosted by Google’s Gmail. Correspondence is used to respond, troubleshoot and handle privacy requests. It may be kept while a request is open or as needed for follow-up, security or legal obligations. Email kosynced@gmail.com to ask about, correct or delete information you have sent. Do not send pairing keys or private health or notification content. The developer cannot remotely access or erase records held only on your devices.

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. These pages have no analytics, contact form, advertising scripts or tracking cookies added by Kosync. Opening an email link starts your mail app. External sites apply their own privacy policies. GitHub Privacy Statement · Google Privacy Policy · Apple Privacy Policy.
