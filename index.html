Kosync Support

Get help connecting your Galaxy Watch to your iPhone, syncing a walk or managing a purchase.

Contact the developer

For app issues, feedback and privacy requests, email John Jacob Koshy.

kosynced@gmail.com

For troubleshooting, include your watch model, iOS and Wear OS versions, Kosync version and what you saw on each device. Keep pairing QR codes, keys, message previews, personal health records and payment details out of your email. If you choose to attach a diagnostic report, review it first.

Common questions

How do I reconnect my saved watch?

Bring the devices close and check that Bluetooth is on. On the watch, open Kosync → Settings → Connection and choose Start connection if it is stopped. On the iPhone, pull down on Overview. For a connection test, open Settings → Setup Guide and go to the final checklist. Keep your existing pairing during troubleshooting.

Why do my walk totals differ?

A Samsung Health workout and a daily activity total cover different periods. Compare daily steps with daily steps for the same date, then check the last measurement time in Kosync. Watch-reported daily calories include resting and active energy. Pull down in Health while connected to request available updates. The watch can deliver readings in batches.

For missing imports, check Kosync’s sensor and activity permissions on the watch and its read and write permissions in Apple Health on the iPhone. New watch health collection and transfer require active Premium access in this version.

How do I enable notification and incoming-call alerts?

In iPhone Settings → Bluetooth, tap the information button beside the watch and enable Share System Notifications if offered. In Kosync on the watch, open Settings → Notifications and enable the alerts you want. Check the watch’s notification permission, vibration settings and Do Not Disturb mode. Use the Premium Setup Guide on the iPhone for the full checklist.

How do I restore or cancel a purchase?

Use Restore Purchases on Kosync’s purchase screen with the Apple Account used to buy it. To cancel monthly renewal, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions → Kosync. A Lifetime purchase is one time. Apple handles billing and refund requests. Request a refund from Apple.

How do I delete data or make a privacy request?

Open Kosync Settings → Legal & Privacy for iPhone controls. Apple Health, the watch and the iPhone each hold different records. See the deletion instructions for the copy you want to remove. To request access to or deletion of information you emailed the developer, use the contact above.
